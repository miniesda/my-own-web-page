About me

✒ I'm a Full Stack Developer who builds interactive web products with React, Node.js, Python and Three.js, and who also brings the eye of a 2D/3D character artist to every project. I love taking on challenging ideas and polishing them until they work and look great. ✒


🚀 I've built educational robotics platforms at JdeRobot–Unibotics and the University of Alicante (UA): full stack features, PostgreSQL databases, automated student evaluation, web gamification, 3D web viewers and simulated robotics environments with O3DE, Docker and URDF. 🚀


📄 My work has been published at Robotics in Education (RiE) 2025, and my master's project was a web and augmented reality app for the Seville, Mallorca and Bilbao metro networks. 📄


📚 I hold a bachelor's degree in Videogame Design and Development and a master's degree in Computer Graphics, Games and VR from Universidad Rey Juan Carlos. 📚


👾 I work with JavaScript, Python, C#, C and Java, with SQL and Elasticsearch on the data side, and I'm comfortable in Unity, 3ds Max, Blender and Photoshop. I follow SOLID principles and SCRUM workflows with Git, GitHub and Trello. 👾


📍 Based in Madrid, open to new opportunities. If you're building web, 3D, VR/AR or game-related products, let's talk! 📍
